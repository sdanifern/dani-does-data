# Overengineering my Singapore Holiday

_Is there a better way to hype yourself up for a holiday than overengineering where you're going to stay so you can eat more and move less? Absolutely not - welcome to my overplanning project._

## About this project

As of today, 28th September 2026, I am dreaming of my 2027 holiday to Singapore while two weeks away from going to Peru. Though I am also excited to go to Peru, Singapore has become this utopic travel destination that has been dangled before my eyes right before having to postpone it due to work-study-life balance reasons.

Earlier this year, completing my End-Point Assessment with BPP University begrudgingly became a priority over deciding which cuisine I'd delve first into upon arriving to the south-east Asian food haven. As an act of defiance following the postponing of my long-awaited trip, I have decided to overengineer this holiday by _empirically establishing the optimal hotel location_ based on food hotspots and transport networks.

As a bonus, you get to observe my neurotic process of working outside working hours to, instead, plan a holiday by calling it "machine leisure" rather than machine learning.
